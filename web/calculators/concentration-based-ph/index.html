pH from hydronium concentration

Estimate concentration-based pH from a positive hydronium concentration in mol/L.

Published · R0

Method and scope

pH ≈ −log₁₀([H₃O⁺]/(1 mol/L)).

Use ph from hydronium concentration for educational calculation or a clearly scoped arithmetic estimate. Intended for Learners, General educational use.

Inputs
  • Hydronium concentration — mol/L, mmol/L
SOURCE AND LIMITS
  • OpenStax, Chemistry: Atoms First 2e, §14.2 pH and pOH; pH = −log[H₃O⁺]. ↗

Assumptions: Uses concentration as an approximation to activity for a dilute aqueous solution. The input is hydronium concentration, not pOH or a measured electrode voltage.

Limitations: Thermodynamic pH is based on activity; concentrated or nonideal solutions require activity corrections.
