WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT MEASURE

Flat geometry, with visible assumptions.

Length uses the ratio between your marked reference pixels and its known length. Area uses that same scale squared and the polygon you mark. Both are approximate 2D estimates. A tilted surface, a reference at another depth, a curved object, a blurry edge, or an unknown reference can make the result misleading.

AI guidance can be mistaken and does not verify measurements. For engineering, construction, medical, legal, or safety decisions, verify dimensions with a calibrated measuring instrument and a qualified professional.
