Sample grade by mass

Calculate contained material as a mass percentage of a user-supplied sample mass.

Published · R0

Method and scope

Mass grade = contained material mass ÷ total sample mass × 100%.

Calculate a simple sample mass fraction for learning or to summarize a provided assay result. Intended for Learners, General geology use.

Inputs
  • Contained material mass — kg, g, t
  • Total sample mass — kg, g, t
SOURCE AND LIMITS
  • U.S. Geological Survey, Scientific Investigations Report 2026–5031, glossary: resource grade is average resource concentration in the ore mass. ↗

Assumptions: Contained material mass and sample mass refer to the same representative sample. Both mass values use compatible units.

Limitations: Representativeness, sampling bias, assay uncertainty, and mineral processing are not assessed.

Method version 1.0.0 · Unit registry and result provenance are shown after calculation. This tool is limited to the documented scope.
