Ohm’s law: solve one missing value

Enter exactly two values to solve the third in a simple resistive DC circuit.

Published · R0

Method and scope

Ohm’s law V = I × R; supply any two of voltage, current, and resistance to calculate the third.

Use Ohm’s law in a classroom example for an ideal ohmic component. Intended for Learners, Electronics hobbyists.

Inputs
  • Voltage — V, mV, kV
  • Current — A, mA
  • Resistance — Ω, kΩ, MΩ
SOURCE AND LIMITS
  • OpenStax, University Physics Volume 2, §9.3, Ohm’s law V = IR for an ohmic material. ↗

Assumptions: The component is treated as an ideal, ohmic resistor under fixed conditions. Voltage and current are entered as non-negative magnitudes; resistance is positive.

Limitations: Many real components are nonlinear or change with temperature and operating point.
