Amount of substance from mass

Convert a sample mass to amount of substance using a molar mass supplied by the user.

Published · R0

Method and scope

Amount n = sample mass m ÷ molar mass M.

Apply n = m ÷ M using a molar mass already determined from a trusted chemical reference. Intended for Learners, Classroom use.

Inputs
  • Sample mass — g, kg, mg
  • Molar mass — g/mol, kg/mol
SOURCE AND LIMITS
  • OpenStax, Chemistry 2e, §3.1, molar mass is mass per mole; amount follows from mass divided by molar mass. ↗

Assumptions: The supplied molar mass is correct for the stated substance and isotopic composition. Mass and molar mass use compatible units.

Limitations: Purity, hydration state, isotopic composition, and measurement uncertainty are not assessed.

Method version 1.0.0 · Unit registry and result provenance are shown after calculation. This tool is limited to the documented scope.
