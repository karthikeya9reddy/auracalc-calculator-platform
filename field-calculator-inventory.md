# Field calculator inventory and dynamic catalog design

**Research snapshot:** 10 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Identify the kinds of calculators people use by field and subfield, and define a traceable method for expanding this into a catalog. The current rough planning estimate is about **2,000 distinct calculation definitions** at eventual full breadth; it is not a verified count or a promise that every candidate is ready to publish. No individual calculator is selected or implemented here.

## What we know about the size

The current taxonomy has **48 top-level field groups**. The field research already identifies broad recurring calculation families; this pass expands those groups into subfields and candidate calculation families, and adds documented multi-step workflows. It does **not** yet have a verified count of unique calculator definitions.

The estimate of about 2,000 is a working planning number, not an evidence-backed forecast. Many fields contain different tasks, methods, populations, codes, jurisdictions, and input/output contracts, but the final count is unknown until leaf records are individually enumerated, deduplicated, sourced, and assigned a definition boundary. Unit systems, languages, themes, presets, and alternate labels are variants—not extra calculators. A distinct method or a materially distinct intended use may justify a separate definition.

## Evidence from this research pass

Authoritative tool collections confirm that real work often spans suites and linked tasks:

- **Civil / transportation / water:** FHWA’s Hydraulic Toolbox lists runoff, channels, channel linings, weirs, inlets, detention, bridge scour, riprap, sediment, and culverts. It supports project files, multiple scenarios, plots, and SI or US customary units, and explicitly says engineers should understand accuracy limits and cross-check outputs. [FHWA Hydraulic Toolbox](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/engineering/hydraulics/software/toolbox404.cfm). FHWA’s broader design-aid page also lists roadway geometry, superelevation, curve widening, profiles, barriers, and traffic tools, with professional verification language. [FHWA Highway Design Tools](https://highways.dot.gov/federal-lands/design/tools)
- **Water resources:** USGS StreamStats combines watershed delineation, basin characteristics, flow statistics, and other spatial analysis; its availability varies by state. [USGS StreamStats](https://www.usgs.gov/streamstats). This is a model for location-aware, data-backed definitions with geographic coverage metadata.
- **Mining:** NIOSH’s mining tools cover roof rating, blast-round design, silica exposure, heat stress, mine-fire smoke simulation, pillar design, injury-cost estimation, and airflow estimation. [NIOSH Mining Tools](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/mining/tools/), [NIOSH Mining Program](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/research-programs/portfolio/mining.html). This validates that “mining calculator” spans engineering, safety, operations, and economics. Ore dilution and recovery also need explicit definitions: sources document multiple accepted dilution bases, which can produce different percentages from the same material. [USGS mine-model report](https://pubs.usgs.gov/sir/2010/5090/h/sir2010-5090h_text.pdf), [AusIMM discussion of dilution definitions](https://www.geokniga.org/bookfiles/geokniga-mineralresourcesandorereserveestimation-theausimmguidetogoodpractice.pdf).
- **Medicine / public health:** CDC StatCalc includes sample-size and power tasks, contingency tables, stratified analysis, risk and odds ratios, chi-square, Fisher exact, Poisson/binomial probability, and matched-pair case-control analysis. [CDC StatCalc guide](https://www.cdc.gov/epiinfo/user-guide/statcalc/statcalcintro.html). NIDDK’s eGFR calculator shows why population and method variants matter: adult and pediatric equations differ, and the result is an estimate whose limitations must be presented. [NIDDK eGFR calculators](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/professionals/clinical-tools-patient-management/kidney-disease/laboratory-evaluation/estimated-gfr-calculators/adults-pediatrics).
- **Agriculture:** NRCS irrigation guidance connects flow, time, applied depth, and area, then extends into soil-water capacity, crop water use, irrigation efficiency, and scheduling. [NRCS Irrigation Guide](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-11/WA-Irrigation-Guide_4.pdf). Its irrigation energy tool estimates pumping-energy savings, while its nitrogen and tillage tools connect agronomy to farm costs. [NRCS energy tools](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/conservation-basics/energy). University Extension sources provide nutrient-management, fertilizer-blending, manure-value, and planting-rate tools. [UMN nutrient calculator](https://apps.extension.umn.edu/calculators/crop/index.html), [Penn State fertilizer calculator](https://extension.psu.edu/fertilizer-calculator).
- **Environmental / household utilities / forestry:** EPA tools span water and energy savings, greenhouse-gas inventories/equivalencies, pollution-prevention cost savings, and pollutant reductions; the EPA describes estimates as approximate where appropriate and identifies when they are unsuitable for formal inventories. [EPA P2 tools](https://www.epa.gov/p2/pollution-prevention-tools-and-calculators), [EPA GHG inventory tool](https://www.epa.gov/statelocalenergy/local-greenhouse-gas-inventory-tool), [EPA WaterSense method](https://www.epa.gov/watersense/how-watersense-calculator-works). NOAA coastal tools link observations to sea-level projections and thresholds. [NOAA Sea Level Analysis Tool](https://www.coast.noaa.gov/digitalcoast/tools/slat.html). US Forest Service’s forest carbon model distinguishes scenarios and states that simulation results are planning aids, not substitutes for measurement. [USFS Forest Sector Carbon Calculator](https://research.fs.usda.gov/pnw/products/dataandtools/forest-sector-carbon-calculator).
- **Workplace safety / manufacturing:** OSHA’s heat calculator takes measured WBGT, workload, acclimatization, and clothing into account; its technical manual also documents duration-weighted noise dose calculations. [OSHA Heat Stress Calculator](https://www.osha.gov/heat-exposure/calculator), [OSHA Noise Technical Manual](https://www.osha.gov/otm/section-3-health-hazards/chapter-5). NIST materials describe tolerance-stack methods including linear propagation, nonlinear propagation, numerical integration, and Monte Carlo simulation, showing why manufacturing calculations need method choice and uncertainty metadata. [NIST tolerance analysis](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/IR/nistir6223.pdf).
- **Buildings / energy:** DOE Home Energy Score uses assessor-collected data about envelope, systems, and floor area with local weather; the report estimates annual use and savings, rather than treating a simple universal equation as a building model. [DOE methodology](https://betterbuildingssolutioncenter.energy.gov/home-energy-score/home-energy-score-methodology).
- **Cloud / software operations:** AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud all use service-specific workload inputs and current region/service pricing rather than a single generic cost equation. AWS supports grouped estimates and exposes assumptions; Azure separates service, region, tier, and monthly usage. [AWS calculator guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/pricing-calculator/latest/userguide/getting-started.html), [Azure calculator guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/costs/pricing-calculator), [Google Cloud cost estimation](https://cloud.google.com/products/calculator).
- **AI / hardware:** AI model sizing is not just parameter count: weight precision, active input/output data, intermediate activations, KV cache, batch size, and parallelism affect memory. [NVIDIA GPU memory guide](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/gpu-memory-essentials-for-ai-performance/), [NVIDIA inference memory guide](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/mastering-llm-techniques-inference-optimization/). Model-quality definitions such as precision/recall also depend on task and threshold. [scikit-learn precision-recall guide](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/auto_examples/model_selection/plot_precision_recall.html).
- **Networks:** IP range and subnet definitions must follow standards such as CIDR rather than legacy class assumptions. [IETF RFC 4632](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4632).
- **VFX / CGI:** Production tools show a connected task set: frames/timecode, retiming, frame chunking, render wall time, farm capacity, render cost, and storage. [CGI Tools calculator library](https://www.cgtools.app/calculators). The existing [VFX/CGI motion research](vfx-cgi-motion-research.md) expands creative and motion-specific workflows. Treat current commercial calculator pages as evidence that the task exists, not as formula authority.
- **Astronomy / sky identification:** IAU defines 88 constellations as bounded sky areas, while NASA describes location-and-time-specific sky maps. [IAU Constellations](https://www.iau.org/IAU/Astronomy-FAQs/Constellations.aspx), [NASA Skywatching FAQ](https://science.nasa.gov/skywatching/faq/). Gaia supplies positions, parallax, proper motion, radial velocity, and brightness data, and Astrometry.net documents plate solving for astronomical images. [Gaia archive](https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/), [Astrometry.net](https://astrometry.net/doc/net/api.html). This supports a research branch under physics for sky mapping, star/planet identification, telescope setup, celestial mechanics, and cosmological-model calculations; it does not establish global demand or justify camera-based scientific claims without alignment and validation.
- **Shared measurement foundation:** NIST documents exact and approximate conversion, significant digits, and delaying rounding until the final result. [NIST unit-conversion guidance](https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/metric-si/unit-conversion). NIST also provides an uncertainty-evaluation tool, demonstrating uncertainty as a calculation family in its own right. [NIST uncertainty resources](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Uncertainty/index.html).
- **Business / tax / household finance:** SBA explicitly uses break-even analysis and identifies its formula and limits. [SBA business planning](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/plan-your-business/). IRS withholding is a multi-stage estimate tied to tax year, filing context, income, adjustments, deductions, credits, and payments. [IRS estimator FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/tax-withholding-estimator-faqs). CFPB defines debt-to-income as a ratio and notes lender limits vary by product. [CFPB DTI guidance](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-debt-to-income-ratio-en-1791/).

These sources prove task and method existence; they do **not** prove worldwide search volume, current competitor saturation, or that every candidate is suitable for our product. Demand and competition need their own query-level evidence.

## Catalog hierarchy and definition boundary

Use this hierarchy:

```text
Field group
  └─ Field / specialty
      └─ Subfield
          └─ User task (what decision or work is being done?)
              └─ Calculation family (method and output meaning)
                  └─ Versioned calculation definition
                      └─ Workflow node(s), if chained with other definitions
```

A candidate becomes a **distinct calculation definition** when it has a materially distinct calculation method, intended result, population, jurisdiction/code interpretation, or operating scope that changes meaning or safe applicability. It is usually a **variant** when only language, units, input order, display precision, color/theme, or a default preset changes. A workflow is not a new formula: it is a directed sequence of approved calculation definitions with typed handoffs and preserved provenance.

### Proposed record schema

Every candidate gets a stable ID and these fields before it can move to implementation:

| Record group | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Identity | `calculator_id`, human name, aliases/search phrases, status, version, created/reviewed dates |
| Taxonomy | field group, specialty, subfield, task, related definitions, workflow membership |
| User intent | user role, context, decision supported, intended use, excluded use, geography/language |
| Method | computation type (equation, conversion, statistical method, solver, lookup/model, simulation), method name, equation/algorithm, variable dictionary, sign and convention rules |
| Contract | typed inputs and outputs, dimensions, accepted unit families, ranges, optional/required rules, defaults with rationale, validation and diagnostics |
| Result quality | internal numeric type, precision, rounding, significant-figure policy, tolerance/uncertainty, convergence or confidence behavior |
| Evidence | primary/authoritative source, edition/version/section/page, formula transcription reviewer, independently derived reference cases, method limitations |
| Governance | risk class from [calculation assurance policy](calculation-assurance-policy.md), qualified reviewer(s), owner, jurisdiction/regulatory assessment, release status |
| Demand and competition | query set, region/date, demand signals, result-page sample, direct competitors, unmet need, confidence—kept distinct from method evidence |
| Maintenance | volatile data/services, source freshness trigger, review cadence, deprecation trigger, monthly upkeep estimate, named change owner |
| Product behavior | visual/diagram requirements, accessibility alternative, related next steps, export/share behavior, privacy handling |

Status values should be controlled: `discovered → scoped → source-found → independently-reviewed → specified → verified → published`, with `rejected`, `stale`, `paused`, or `retired` as explicit side states. Only `verified` can be published under the [assurance policy](calculation-assurance-policy.md).

## Dynamic calculation architecture for a large catalog

The goal is not a thousand hand-built pages or one AI prompt that guesses formulas. The goal is a small number of reviewed calculation patterns plus versioned, declarative definitions and explicit workflow composition.

1. **Shared typed calculation core:** deterministic computation functions operate on quantities, dimensions, units, uncertainty, and structured diagnostics. UI, SEO, AI, and visual layers cannot change numeric logic.
2. **Calculation definition registry:** each reviewed definition declares its schema, method/version, sources, risk class, limits, reference cases, and presentation metadata. A new calculator should mainly be a reviewed definition plus a tested method adapter.
3. **Method adapters:** support algebraic formulas, conversions, statistics, numerical solvers, standards-based checks, data-backed estimates, and simulations. Each adapter type has its own validation and failure rules; do not force a simulation into a simple formula shape.
4. **Typed workflow graph:** calculations can feed later calculations only when units, meanings, and provenance match. Each node stores its exact definition/version and input origin; a changed upstream assumption marks downstream results stale and recalculates in dependency order.
5. **Context packs:** localization, unit preferences, jurisdiction, standard edition, data release, and domain vocabulary are separate from the core equation. Context packs are versioned and can narrow or block unsupported uses.
6. **Scenario runner:** compare named user-controlled cases side-by-side; never hide a changed assumption. Provide sensitivity plots only when the method supports the interpretation.
7. **Visual adapters:** connect 2D/3D geometry, graphs, timelines, and photo/camera measurements through explicit input ports. Visual measurement carries calibration and uncertainty; low-confidence inputs block consequential downstream use.
8. **Sensor/vision layer:** camera frames, tracking, calibration, depth, GNSS, inertial samples, orientation, time/location, and astronomy datasets produce provenance-bearing measurements with uncertainty and quality flags. Those measurements feed the same reviewed definitions as manual inputs; unsupported sensor quality blocks downstream use. Keep video motion analysis, camera measurement, astronomy alignment, and calibrated colorimetry as separate method adapters. See [camera and sensor calculator research](camera-sensor-calculator-research.md).
9. **AI assistance boundary:** AI may map a user’s words to candidate tasks, explain approved metadata, and help users navigate a workflow. It must show the matched definition, ask users to confirm extracted inputs, and call the reviewed engine. It cannot create formulas, silently choose standards, overrule failed checks, or invent a result.
10. **SEO and user discovery:** pages are generated from reviewed definitions plus original explanatory content. Similar aliases and unit variants point to one canonical definition; substantive regional/method variants have separate pages only when content and applicability truly differ.
11. **Low-maintenance operation:** static/pre-rendered content and client-side deterministic calculations where suitable; managed hosting/CDN; versioned content; automated source-expiry reminders; one-click pause/rollback; no sensitive input logging by default. Add servers only for a reviewed need such as large simulations, accounts, or controlled dynamic datasets.

## Dynamic multi-calculator workflows to research

The following illustrate how one task can require several calculators. They are candidate journey maps, not validated engineering designs. Workflows should preserve every step and offer branching, save/reopen, scenario compare, and an editable result sheet.

| User task | Likely linked steps | Trust/product requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Plan a small building project | dimensions/area → takeoff → material/waste quantities → local unit prices → labor/time → cost scenarios | Keep measured quantity separate from price assumptions; identify region/date; show missing items instead of implying a complete quote. |
| Evaluate a drainage concept | rainfall/basin inputs → runoff estimate → channel/culvert/inlet capacity → detention scenario → report | Jurisdiction, return period, data source, assumptions, and independent professional verification travel through the whole chain. |
| Evaluate a mine production scenario | grade/tonnage → explicitly defined dilution/recovery → blend/mass balance → equipment cycle capacity → energy/cost sensitivity | Do not reuse a “dilution %” without the same denominator; keep economic assumptions user-supplied or source-dated. |
| Plan irrigation and farm input | crop/area → water depth/volume → flow/time → pumping energy/cost → nutrient/fertilizer quantity → seasonal comparison | Soil, crop stage, local recommendations, water availability, efficiency, and product labels remain visible. |
| Estimate a cloud application | requests/workload → compute/storage/network → redundancy/backup/logging → region pricing → monthly and annual scenarios | Price-list timestamp, usage model, tax/discount exclusions, and estimate-vs-bill caveat. |
| Size an AI inference service | model/precision → weight and KV-cache memory → batch/context assumptions → hardware count → throughput → provider/hardware cost | Show heuristic versus benchmark-derived inputs; uncertain estimates stay ranges and cannot promise fit/performance. |
| Prepare a VFX shot handoff | frames/timecode → retime/conform → render work/chunk plan → farm wall time → storage/transfer → budget | Exact FPS and drop-frame semantics; project settings preserved; distinguish CPU time from wall-clock time. |
| Compare a household purchase | unit-price comparison → tax/discount → financing payment → energy/maintenance scenario | Keep local fees, rates, and product claims dated; show total cost and scenario assumptions. |
| Prepare a public-health study | study design → sample size/power → data table → effect/rate calculations → uncertainty and stratification | Named method, denominator, study assumptions, and interpretation limits; no diagnosis or individual treatment recommendation. |

## How to continue the research without inflating the inventory

Research each field in **clusters**, and retain a completed source-and-demand sheet per cluster:

1. **Discover tasks:** authoritative manuals, professional workflows, public institutional tools, training texts, software manuals, practitioner interviews, and search phrasing.
2. **Split subfields:** use the terminology workers actually use; include alternate names and cross-field tasks, but link synonyms to one concept.
3. **Define calculation families:** write one sentence for the real job, method, input/output meaning, and what changes its scope. Keep separate methods separate; merge cosmetic variants.
4. **Find authoritative evidence:** primary standards, government/institutional manuals, peer-reviewed methods, official product documentation, or university extension material. Record page/section and edition; do not cite another calculator as proof of correctness.
5. **Check demand and saturation separately:** query families by locale and date; sample organic results and specialist software; record user intent, visible competition, missing capabilities, and confidence. One official calculator proves use, not search demand.
6. **Map chains:** interview or observe where users carry one result into another sheet, website, CAD tool, software package, or manual method. Make workflow links based on shared user tasks, not page-view goals.
7. **Apply risk and maintenance gates:** classify consequence before implementation; estimate source volatility, reviewer access, and monthly upkeep. Reject or defer items without a sustainable review owner.
8. **Review coverage:** count unique definitions only after deduplication. Report counts by `discovered`, `source-found`, `specified`, `verified`, and `published`; publish no single “total calculators” number without its counting rule.

## Decisions for the framework

- Keep the **48 field groups** as navigation-level parents for now; add subfields as controlled tags rather than multiplying top-level disciplines.
- Build a **catalog of definitions and workflows**, not a one-page-per-variant SEO factory.
- Treat workflow composition as a core feature: users can save a project, reuse prior values with provenance, branch scenarios, and inspect each intermediate result.
- Offer optional diagrams, unit-aware visuals, photo/camera measurements, and AI navigation only as validated adapters around explicit inputs. AI/image estimates inherit uncertainty and cannot bypass the risk class of downstream tasks.
- Estimate the catalog’s eventual size from deduplicated reviewed definitions. It may exceed 1,000; current evidence supports a large candidate landscape but not a defensible final count.
- Preserve [calculation assurance policy](calculation-assurance-policy.md) as a hard publish gate for all individual calculators and entire chained workflows.
