Service availability from downtime

Calculate time-based availability from an observation period and measured downtime.

Published · R0

Method and scope

Availability = (total observation time − downtime) ÷ total observation time × 100%.

Calculate a historical time-based availability percentage for a stated observation window. Intended for Learners, Operations teams.

Inputs
  • Total observation time — 1
  • Measured downtime — 1
SOURCE AND LIMITS
  • Google Cloud SRE, “Available or not: That is the question,” time-based availability may be measured as uptime / (uptime + downtime). ↗

Assumptions: Observation and downtime values use the same time unit and measurement window. Downtime is measured using the user’s stated service availability definition.

Limitations: A time-based metric may differ from request-based availability.

Method version 1.0.0 · Unit registry and result provenance are shown after calculation. This tool is limited to the documented scope.
