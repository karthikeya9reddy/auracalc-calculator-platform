Gross profit and margin

Calculate gross profit and gross margin from revenue and cost of goods sold.

Published · R0

Method and scope

Gross profit = revenue − cost of goods sold. Gross margin = gross profit ÷ revenue × 100%.

Show the basic relationship between sales revenue, cost of goods sold, and gross margin. Intended for Learners, General business use.

Inputs
  • Revenue for the period — 1
  • Cost of goods sold — 1
SOURCE AND LIMITS
  • OpenStax, Principles of Finance, §5.1, gross margin = gross profit / sales; gross profit is sales less cost of goods sold. ↗

Assumptions: Revenue and cost of goods sold cover the same period and use the same currency. The user supplies the revenue and cost amounts.

Limitations: Revenue must be positive; negative gross profit is allowed and shown as a negative margin.

Method version 1.0.0 · Unit registry and result provenance are shown after calculation. This tool is limited to the documented scope.
