Discount and final price

See the discount amount and price after a stated percentage discount.

Published · R0

Method and scope

Discount = original price × discount rate ÷ 100. Final price = original price − discount.

Calculate a simple percentage discount from a user-entered original price. Intended for Everyday use, Learners.

Inputs
  • Original price — 1
  • Discount — 1
SOURCE AND LIMITS
  • OpenStax, Prealgebra 2e, §6.2 Solve General Applications of Percent. ↗

Assumptions: The discount is from 0% through 100%. Original and final prices use the same currency and scale.

Limitations: Enter the price in any currency; the calculator does not identify the currency or determine whether a promotion applies.

Method version 1.0.0 · Unit registry and result provenance are shown after calculation. This tool is limited to the documented scope.
